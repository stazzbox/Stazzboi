President

The Presidency is the central executive office responsible for national direction, sovereignty, order, unity, cultural continuity, and Stazzerian identity.

Vice President

The Vice President is the principal executive counterpart to the Presidency, providing continuity, coordination, representation, and executive support.

First Lady

LeAnn Stazzland is the current First Lady, Head Stazzlie, leading female representative of the NATION, and presiding ceremonial authority of the Stazzlies. Her responsibilities include presiding over and representing the Stazzlies, supporting cultural continuity, public representation, Stazzerian events and symbolism, and presidential initiatives.

War General

The War General is the primary military and conflict authority responsible for strategic protection, rapid conflict response, security alignment, internal stability, operational defense, and defensive continuity. John B. Stazzland currently holds the office.

First Judge

The First Judge is the senior judicial office associated with the Stazziciary. The SSAC is the formal court responsible for hearing and adjudicating matters under the Stazzerian Framework.

Advisor

The Advisor provides senior strategic counsel, institutional continuity, and high-level guidance to the Presidency. Caro currently holds the office and previously served as first-term Vice President.

Monarch

The Monarch is a distinct office in the Stazzerian hierarchy, with the recognized Monarch/Jester institutional dynamic.
