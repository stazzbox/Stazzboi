“STX 050 — Stazzerian President”

President

The Presidency (STX-050) is the central executive office responsible for national direction, sovereignty, order, unity, cultural continuity, and Stazzerian identity.

“STX 049 — Stazzerian Vice President”

Vice President

The Vice President (STX-049) is the principal executive counterpart to the Presidency, providing continuity, coordination, representation, and executive support.

“STX 014 — First Lady of Stazzeria”

First Lady

LeAnn Stazzland is the current First Lady, Head Stazzlie, leading female representative of the NATION, and presiding ceremonial authority of the Stazzlies. Her responsibilities include presiding over and representing the Stazzlies, supporting cultural continuity, public representation, Stazzerian events and symbolism, and presidential initiatives.

“STX 048 — Stazzerian War General”

War General

The War General (STX-048) is the primary military and conflict authority responsible for strategic protection, rapid conflict response, security alignment, internal stability, operational defense, and defensive continuity. John B. Stazzland currently holds the office.

“STX 047 — Stazzerian Judicial Reference”

First Judge

The First Judge (STX-047) is the senior judicial office associated with the Stazziciary. The SSAC is the formal court responsible for hearing and adjudicating matters under the Stazzerian Framework.

“STX 080 — Stazzerian Advisor”

Advisor

The Advisor (STX-080) provides senior strategic counsel, institutional continuity, and high-level guidance to the Presidency. Caro currently holds the office and previously served as first-term Vice President.

“STX 046 — Stazzerian Monarch”

Monarch

The Monarch (STX-046) is a distinct office in the Stazzerian hierarchy, with the recognized Monarch/Jester institutional dynamic.
