Overview

Kelvin “The Khamelion” Stazzland, professionally known as Stazzboi, is the 50th President of Stazzeria. He serves as the executive head of the nation's government and presides over the Department of Stazz (DOS), the administration responsible for Stazzerian institutional operations and citizen affairs.

Founded April 15, 2017, Stazzeria is organized as a digital republic with physical gatherings. The Department of Stazz was formally established in 2024 during the Stazzerian Renaissance. Its national framework is grounded in its people, records, institutions, and the Stazzex digital archive.

The Presidency & Administration

Stazzland's administration operates through defined offices and relationships rather than the presidency acting alone. Vice President Isaac “BeachyBird” Ochoa works alongside him in the executive administration. Mediator James C., also serving as Interim First Judge, contributes to the resolution of Stazzerian challenges and judicial matters. First Lady LeAnn Stazzland and other Department officials hold their own institutional responsibilities.

The President also holds the Head of Stazz office, connecting presidential leadership with Stazzboi's organizational activities. The official Department of Stazz directory maintains the full office roster and appointments.

Stazzism & Leadership Philosophy

Stazzism promotes belonging, continuous self-improvement, mutual support, personal discipline, and collective responsibility. Stazzland's leadership emphasizes Action over Words: progress demonstrated through participation and execution rather than declarations alone.

Stazzeria's citizenship is participatory and relational. Its institutions provide a framework through which Stazzerians contribute to and preserve a collective identity.
