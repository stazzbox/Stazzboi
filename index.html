About Stazzboi

Stazzboi is the personal brand of Kelvin Stazzland in which the Stazz Universe forms, where Stazzland leads his nation of Stazzeria as its PRESIDENT.

Stazzland is known for his gaming promotion, Stazzology, which primarily focuses on Call of Duty: Mobile (CODM) and gained attention from the Megabites Arcade collaboration Bites & Battles, hosted in Hemet, California.

Stazzland and his female costars, known as The Stazzlies, collaborate in content, while the Stazzerian index known as The Stazzex records and documents the process, becoming Stazzerian history.
