Stazzboi

Kelvin Stazzland

Kelvin Stazzland (born September 15, 1998), professionally known as Stazzboi, is a Stazzerian-American independent recording artist, entertainer, content creator, and digital entrepreneur based in California. He serves as President of Stazzeria, an independently established digital republic, and is recognized for documenting his personal, creative, and presidential experiences through his social entertainment persona, The Khamelion.

The Khamelion represents Stazzland’s adaptability across social environments, personalities, and experiences, reflecting the characteristics of a social chameleon. Through content creation, livestreaming, public interactions, and entertainment, he documents his activities while connecting his artistic identity with his personal life and leadership within Stazzeria.

As Stazzboi, Stazzland pursues music and entertainment; as President, he oversees Stazzeria’s institutional development; and through The Khamelion, he brings these experiences to audiences through personality-driven content and social engagement.
