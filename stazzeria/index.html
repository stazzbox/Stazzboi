President

The Presidency is the central executive office. Within the Stazzerian Framework, the President is responsible for national direction, sovereignty, order, unity, cultural continuity, and the preservation of Stazzerian identity.

Vice President

The Vice President is the principal executive counterpart to the Presidency: a continuity, coordination, representation, and executive-support role. The current office is held by Isaac “BeachyBird” Ochoa, whose established creator-and-cameraman partnership with the President is part of the office’s institutional history.

First Lady

The current First Lady is LeAnn Stazzland. The office is not treated merely as a spouse title: within Stazzerian canon she is the Head Stazzlie, the leading female representative of the NATION, and the presiding ceremonial authority of the Stazzlies.

Her responsibilities therefore include presiding over and representing the Stazzlies, supporting their cultural participation and continuity, representing the Presidency publicly, participating in Stazzerian events and symbolism, and supporting presidential initiatives. This combines the public, ceremonial, cultural, and advocacy functions commonly associated with First Spouse roles with the specific Stazzlerian duty of leading the Stazzlies.

The current record identifies LeAnn as the second and current First Lady, following Amy Stazzland during the first presidential term.

War General

The War General is the primary military and conflict authority of Stazzeria. The office is responsible for strategic protection, rapid response during conflicts, security alignment, internal stability, operational defense, and long-term defensive continuity. The current record identifies John B. Stazzland as War General.

First Judge

The First Judge is presented as the senior judicial office associated with the Stazziciary. The Stazziciary is the judicial institution of Stazzeria, while the SSAC is its formal court responsible for hearing and adjudicating matters under the Stazzerian Framework. No individual First Judge is identified here without a canonical officeholder record.

Advisor

The Advisor provides senior strategic counsel, institutional continuity, and high-level guidance to the Presidency. Caro currently holds the office and previously served as the first Stazzerian Vice President.

Monarch

The Monarch is a distinct office in the Stazzerian hierarchy. Existing Stazzerian material describes a Monarch / Jester dynamic in which the Monarch can transition into a temporary Jester state under disciplinary conditions before returning to full structural status.

Historical Reference

Stazzerian history records Kelvin Stazzland as the current President, with the second presidential term beginning October 31, 2025. That current administration records LeAnn as First Lady and Isaac “BeachyBird” as Vice President. The Stazzex serves as the archival reference system connecting these offices to their histories and definitions.
