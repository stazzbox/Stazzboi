Department of Stazz (DOS)

Government Institution / Executive Administration

The Department of Stazz (DOS) is the central governmental and executive administrative institution of Stazzeria. It comprises the recognized offices and leadership structure responsible for administering Stazzerian governance, coordinating institutional affairs, and carrying out the principles of Stazzerian Presidential Collectivism.

Motto: Ordo in Chao — Order in Chaos.
