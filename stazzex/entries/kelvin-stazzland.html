Kelvin Stazzland

Kelvin “The Khamelion” Stazzland, professionally known as Stazzboi, is a Stazzerian-American content creator, entertainer, producer, musician, and Internet personality. He is the founder and President of Stazzeria and the principal creator behind the Stazzerian cultural framework, including STAZZsociety, Stazzism, The Stazzex, and Stazzology.
