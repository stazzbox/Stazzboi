STX-050 · Title

Stazzerian President

The President of Stazzeria [STX-050] is the highest executive office within Stazzeria [STX-009] and serves as head of state, government, and national direction. Under Stazzerian Presidential Collectivism [STX-051], the President does not operate as the sole source of the collective will. The office provides executive leadership while collaborating with Stazzerian citizens [STX-008] by receiving proposals, considering the interests and needs of residents, coordinating institutional action, and implementing the collective direction of Stazzeria.

Kelvin Stazzland [STX-002] is the current Stazzerian President and founding figure of Stazzeria. His presidential role is therefore presented as a collaborative executive position: he establishes direction, works with residents, and maintains continuity while the Stazzerian collective participates in shaping the will of the nation.
