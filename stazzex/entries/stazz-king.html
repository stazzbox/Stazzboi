Stazz King

Stazzerian King or Stazz King is a formal Stazzerian title reserved solely for Kelvin Stazzland, including when he is not occupying public office. The title represents his masculine royal identity within the Stazzland household, with StazzQueen serving as its feminine counterpart. Together, the titles reference the Stazzland household, commonly known as The Stazzerian Castle.
