Stazz King

STX 078 • Personal Title

Stazz King — A permanent and exclusive personal title reserved solely for Kelvin Stazzland (Stazzboi), regardless of whether he currently occupies the office of President of Stazzeria. The title represents his position as the principal figure of the Stazzland household, and is therefore distinct from any governmental or executive office.

The Stazz King and StazzQueen titles collectively refer to the principal members of the Stazzland household, which is commonly known within Stazzerian culture as the Stazzerian Castle. Together, the titles represent the household’s central royal identity and personal legacy, while remaining distinct from the formal institutions of Stazzerian government.
