Stazzerian Castle

The Stazzerian Castle is the recognized name for the Stazzland household, representing the familial and romantic household structure associated with Kelvin Stazzland, the Stazz King, and the StazzQueen. It is closely connected to The Stazzerian and Stazzian Estate and serves as a symbolic representation of the Stazzland household within Stazzerian culture and history. It is personally referred to by Stazzland as “The Castle,” “My Castle,” and “Our Castle.”
