STX-002

Kelvin Stazzland

President of Stazzeria · Head of State of Stazzeria

Person

Kelvin “The Khamelion” Stazzland, professionally known as Stazzboi, is a Stazzerian-American content creator, entertainer, producer, musician, and Internet personality. He is the founder, President of Stazzeria, and Head of State of Stazzeria and the principal creator behind the Stazzerian cultural framework, including STAZZsociety, Stazzism, The Stazzex, and Stazzology.
