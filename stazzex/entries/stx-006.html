BeachyBird

Person

BeachyBird is the professional name of Isaac Ochoa, a content creator and videographer closely associated with Stazzeria. He serves as the Stazzerian Vice President and is a close friend and creative collaborator of Kelvin Stazzland, professionally known as Stazzboi. Together, they form a creator-and-cameraman duo associated with Stazzerian media and content creation.
