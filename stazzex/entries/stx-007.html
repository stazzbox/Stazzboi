STX-007

Birthright

Citizenship

Birthright refers to Birthright Stazzerian citizenship, a Stazzerian citizenship classification granted through direct lineage. A Birthright Stazzerian is recognized as a citizen of Stazzeria by virtue of being born into the established Stazzerian lineage.
