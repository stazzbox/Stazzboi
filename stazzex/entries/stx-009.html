Stazzeria

Nation / Collective Identity

Stazzeria is the nation and collective identity of Stazzerians, founded by Kelvin Stazzland on April 15, 2017. The Digital Republic of Stazzeria was established in November 2025. Its capital is Kavik, and its primary digital territory and enduring archival infrastructure is The Stazzex.
