STX-017

“If the answer is obvious, implement it.”

Principle

“If the answer is obvious, implement it.” is a Stazzist principle and call to action emphasizing decisive execution when a clear solution is already apparent. It advocates implementing an obvious and sufficiently understood answer rather than unnecessarily delaying action through overanalysis or hesitation. The principle is applicable broadly and is not limited to content creation.
