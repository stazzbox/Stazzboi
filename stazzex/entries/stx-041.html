STX-041

Stazzerian Assembly

Institution

The Stazzerian Assembly is the fundamental collective formation of Stazzerians, beginning whenever two or more Stazzerians come together, thereby forming Stazzeria at its most basic social level. At an elevated level, the Stazzerian Assembly refers to a cultural gathering of Stazzerians for shared participation, discussion, community, and expression of Stazzerian identity.
