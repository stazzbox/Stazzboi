STX-051

Stazzerian Presidential Collectivism

Governance System

Stazzerian Presidential Collectivism is the governing system of Stazzeria in which the Stazzerian President serves as the head of state, government, and national direction while the collective participation of Stazzerians contributes to determining the will of the nation. The President is responsible for executing, directing, and safeguarding that collective will rather than governing solely through unilateral authority.
