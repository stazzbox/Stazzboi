STX-063

Stazzism

Doctrine

Stazzism is a Stazzist doctrine developed by Kelvin Stazzland and the ethical, social, and philosophical foundation of Stazzeria. It emphasizes kindness, mutual support, continuous improvement, loyalty, family bonds, self-governance, responsibility, discipline, collective accountability, informed decision-making, and Action Over Words: implementing knowledge responsibly instead of relying on declarations alone.
