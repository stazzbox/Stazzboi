Stazzist Doctrine

Doctrine

A Stazzist Doctrine is a formal system of principles, beliefs, and guiding philosophies associated with Stazzist thought and practice. Stazzism, developed by Kelvin Stazzland, is a Stazzist doctrine that establishes the ethical, social, and philosophical foundation of Stazzeria. It emphasizes Action Over Words, informed decision-making, responsible implementation, kindness, mutual support, continuous improvement, loyalty, self-governance, discipline, and collective accountability.
