STX-068

Stazzology

Brand

Stazzology is an independent gaming and competitive entertainment brand associated with Stazzboi, primarily focused on Call of Duty: Mobile (CODM). It develops gaming content and organizes or promotes competitive events, teams, tournaments, and related media, including Stazzboi Gaming. Its activities also include promotions such as the April 11, 2026 Bites & Battles Street Fighter 6 tournament at Megabites Arcade in Hemet, California. Stazzology is not a department, subordinate institution, or member of Stazzeria; its connection to Stazzeria is through Stazzboi and The Stazz Universe.
