STX-070

Supporter

Citizenship

Stazzerian Supporter refers to a Stazzerian citizenship classification for an individual who is recognized as a supporter or participant of Stazzeria without possessing Birthright or Adopted Stazzerian citizenship. Supporter status functions as the equivalent of a Stazzerian visa, providing a recognized connection to Stazzeria without establishing birthright or adopted citizenship.
