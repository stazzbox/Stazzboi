STX-075

Zits Conflict

Historical / History

The Zits Conflict was a social confrontation in Stazzerian history involving President Kelvin Stazzland, an opposing individual publicly designated as Zits, First Lady LeAnn Stazzland, and John B. Stazzland. The confrontation began after remarks directed against Stazzeria escalated into an exchange of M&M pellets. Zits initiated the exchange, President Stazzland responded, and John B. joined the Stazzerian side, contributing significantly to its victory. The conflict included a temporary truce that was subsequently violated before hostilities ceased. First Lady LeAnn Stazzland played a central role in resolving the confrontation and establishing peace. John B. was subsequently appointed Stazzerian War General. Within Stazzerian history, the conflict is remembered as a decisive Stazzerian victory and an example of loyalty, collective defense, symbolic confrontation, and diplomatic resolution.
