STX-076

StazzQueen

Title

StazzQueen is a formal title within the Stazzian and Stazzerian household structure, referring to the recognized female counterpart to Stazz King. Associated with the Stazzland household, commonly referred to as The Stazzerian Castle, the title represents the feminine identity within the household. StazzQueen is a personal designation distinct from the institutional office of First Lady of Stazzeria; holding the office of First Lady does not automatically confer the title of StazzQueen.
