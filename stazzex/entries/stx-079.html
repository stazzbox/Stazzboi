STX-079

Stazzerian Castle

Place / Household

The Stazzerian Castle is the recognized name and enduring concept of the Stazzland household, representing the familial, romantic, and personal household structure associated with Kelvin Stazzland, the Stazz King, and the StazzQueen. Originating from the foundational Stazzland household, the Castle symbolizes affection, stability, continuity, and the ongoing development of the household within Stazzerian culture and history. Closely associated with The Stazzian Estate, the Castle continues as a household concept beyond its original formation. Kelvin Stazzland personally refers to it as “The Castle,” “My Castle,” and “Our Castle”.
