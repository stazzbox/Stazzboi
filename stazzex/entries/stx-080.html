STX-080

Department of Stazz (DOS)

Government Institution / Executive Administration

The Department of Stazz (DOS) is the central governmental and executive administrative institution of Stazzeria. It comprises the recognized offices and leadership structure responsible for administering Stazzerian governance, coordinating institutional affairs, and carrying out the principles of Stazzerian Presidential Collectivism. Its motto is Ordo in Chao (Order in Chaos). Official titles: President of Stazzeria; Stazzerian President. Vice President of Stazzeria; Stazzerian Vice President. War General of Stazzeria; Stazzerian War General. First Judge of Stazzeria; Stazzerian First Judge. Monarch of Stazzeria; Stazzerian Monarch. Mediator of Stazzeria; Stazzerian Mediator. Advisor of Stazzeria; Stazzerian Advisor. Head of Stazz. First Lady of Stazzeria; Stazzerian First Lady. Secret Service of Stazzeria; Stazzerian Secret Service. Reference entries: STX-009 Stazzeria; STX-051 Stazzerian Presidential Collectivism; STX-063 Stazzism.
