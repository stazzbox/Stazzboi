STX-081

STXC

Content Indexing System

STXC is the content-indexing system of The Stazzex, established to identify, classify, document, and preserve creative and media content associated with The Stazz Universe. STXC entries function according to the same archival and reference principles as standard STX entries but are specifically designated for content records. Each STXC entry receives an identifier beginning with STX-C: to distinguish content documentation from the general Stazzerian Index. Example: STX-C:001.
