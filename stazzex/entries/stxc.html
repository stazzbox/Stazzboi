STX-081

STXC

Content Indexing System

STXC is the content-indexing system of The Stazzex, established to identify, classify, document, and preserve creative and media content associated with The Stazz Universe.

STXC records function according to the archival and reference principles of standard STX entries, but are specifically designated for content rather than general subjects.

Identifier format

Each content entry uses the prefix STX-C: followed by its record number; for example, STX-C:001. This is a format example and not a claim that the first content entry has already been issued.

Content metadata

Individual records can document a content title, creator, date, media type, description, official media URL, affiliated brand, and related STX entries.
