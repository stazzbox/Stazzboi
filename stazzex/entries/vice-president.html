STX 049 · Title / Executive Office

Stazzerian Vice President

Who Holds the Office

The Stazzerian Vice President is currently Isaac “BeachyBird” Ochoa, a creator and videographer closely associated with Stazzeria. Within the Stazzerian Framework, BeachyBird is recognized not only by his formal office but also by his established relationship with President Kelvin Stazzland and his role as the President’s right hand.

Vice Presidents of Stazzeria

The Vice Presidency has been held by two members of the Stazzland administration. Caro established the office as its first officeholder, followed by Isaac “BeachyBird” Ochoa, who currently serves as Vice President.

1. Caro — First Stazzerian Vice President

Predecessor: None · Successor: Isaac “BeachyBird” Ochoa

Caro was the first Stazzerian Vice President and the first female Vice President of Stazzeria. She served as Vice President under the Stazzland administration, establishing the office as a central part of the Stazzerian executive structure.

Caro later transitioned from the Vice Presidency into her present role as Stazzerian Advisor, where she serves as a senior strategic counselor within the Department of Stazz. During Caro’s presidential household, JC held the title Second Gentleman of Stazzeria.

2. Isaac “BeachyBird” Ochoa — Current Stazzerian Vice President

Predecessor: Caro · Successor: N/A

Isaac “BeachyBird” Ochoa is the current Stazzerian Vice President, serving under the Stazzland administration. He succeeded Caro in the office and serves as President Kelvin Stazzland’s right hand and principal executive counterpart.

What the Office Is

Stazzerian Vice President is the second-highest executive role within Stazzeria and serves as the principal deputy and executive counterpart to the Stazzerian President. The Vice President assists in directing Stazzerian affairs, supports continuity of leadership, represents the Presidency, and serves as a central coordinating figure within the executive structure, including direct oversight of the Stazzerian Monarch.

Why Isaac Ochoa Holds the Office

Isaac Ochoa’s place as Vice President is rooted in his established position alongside Kelvin Stazzland as a close friend, trusted collaborator, and creative partner. The Stazzian Framework identifies their partnership as a creator-and-cameraman relationship that developed into a central executive alliance. In that structure, BeachyBird functions as the President’s right hand: a trusted counterpart who helps translate presidential direction into coordinated action and provides continuity, perspective, and operational support.

The office therefore represents more than a generic deputy position. It formalizes BeachyBird’s established relationship to the President and his place within the leadership structure of Stazzeria.

Executive Relationship

The Vice President operates in direct partnership with President Kelvin Stazzland. The two are connected through both the governmental structure of Stazzeria and their longstanding creator collaboration. This relationship forms a central pillar of the Stazzerian executive model, with the Vice President serving as the President’s closest executive counterpart and operational sounding board.

Authority and Responsibilities

  • Serves as principal executive support to the President.
  • Supports direction and coordination of Stazzerian affairs.
  • Provides executive continuity, stability, and cooperation.
  • Represents and supports presidential initiatives.
  • Participates in Stazzerian events, symbolism, and national identity.
  • Maintains direct supervisory authority over the Stazzerian Monarch within the established hierarchy.
