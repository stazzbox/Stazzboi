Stazzerian Vice President

Stazzerian Vice President is the second-highest executive role within Stazzeria and serves as the principal deputy to the Stazzerian President. The Vice President assists in directing Stazzerian affairs and serves as the central coordinating figure within the executive structure, including direct oversight of the Stazzerian Monarch.
